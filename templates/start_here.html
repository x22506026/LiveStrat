How to read LiveStrat labels

The app keeps the final wording simple, then shows the evidence underneath.

Positive

Evidence is supportive. The market or strategy view is leaning in a useful direction.

Mixed

Evidence is not clear. The safest interpretation is to wait or compare more data.

Negative

Evidence is weak or risky. The app should avoid making a strong bullish claim.
