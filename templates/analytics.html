Evidence and analytics

How LiveStrat strategies are validated, and what the evidence says.

Each strategy is tested with chronological splits, walk-forward folds, and multiple performance metrics. None of the methods peek at future data.

How the strategies are validated

The four pillars of LiveStrat's evaluation methodology.

1

Time-aware splits

Data is split chronologically into four time-aware partitions: train, calibrate, validate, and test. No shuffling and no future leakage; every fold respects the arrow of time.

2

Walk-forward folds

The model is retrained on an expanding window as new candles arrive. Each fold's test set is data the model has never seen during training.

3

Multiple metrics

Strategies are scored on accuracy, balanced accuracy, macro-F1, Sharpe ratio, max drawdown, and excess return over buy-and-hold. Accuracy alone is misleading.

4

Ablation study

Each data source (futures, news, on-chain, TVL) is added in sequence to measure its individual contribution. The output is honest about which layers actually help and which do not.
