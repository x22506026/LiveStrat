What LiveStrat offers

A focused workspace for checking market history, strategy performance, and the extra context that may support or challenge a trading view.

Market history first View recent price paths, 24h moves, volatility, activity, and futures structure for the tracked assets.
Strategy performance Use preset strategy pages and evaluation summaries based on saved historical model runs.
Sentiment and on-chain context Check where news, network data, and ecosystem TVL are available, and where the platform should rely mainly on market data.
Evidence checks Review accuracy, returns, rolling validation, source coverage, and data freshness before trusting a signal.
